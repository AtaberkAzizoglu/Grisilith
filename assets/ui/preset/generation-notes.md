# Preset journal image assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool (no API/CLI fallback).
The four final PNG assets are stored beside this file.

## Prompt briefs

- `journal_table.png`: A top-down medieval fantasy preparation desk matching Grisilith's painterly candlelit wood, bronze and parchment style. Three straight rectangular UI surfaces: a narrow parchment list on the left and two large flat open notebook pages in the center and right. Keep all writing surfaces blank, unobstructed and square to the camera; put props and detail at the margins. No baked-in text, characters, controls or interface frames.
- `paper_button.png`: A simple horizontal cream parchment control with a fine dark sepia ink border and restrained ornamental corners, matching the notebook. Flat front view, empty center, no text, no metal projections, genuine transparent background.
- `paper_popup.png`: A rectangular cream paper dropdown/tooltip surface with a fine sepia ink outline, subtle texture and generous blank interior. Flat front view, no text, genuine transparent background.
- `paper_scrollbar.png`: A coordinated slender vertical parchment-and-sepia scrollbar rail and a separate compact paper thumb, aligned upright, restrained hand-drawn detail, no blue gems, no text, genuine transparent background. Runtime crops the separate rail and thumb from the sheet.

Text remains rendered by the game so names, descriptions, translations and alignment stay editable. The paper controls are shared between Preset Manager and the run preparation screen.
