EDITS
Excerpts, mono/44.1kHz PCM16 conversion, pitch/speed and EQ, short fades,
occasional vocal + foley layers. Some full-length cues use repeated footstep/air
textures and duration fitting (BC1-3 1.15s, CJ1-3 0.55s). Others are short onsets.
Egg options are five edits of one real egg recording, sometimes with fine debris.
Swarm Being is a passive: suggestions are short mitigation/deflection cues, not a
permanent loop. Split suggestions are brief widening swarm textures.
Gorilla stomp options include natural foley and designed bass/body impacts;
they are sound designs, not recordings of an actual gorilla striking earth.
Peak normalized to -12dBFS, not perceived loudness matched. Full recipes included.
No paid pack, no animal injury/death recordings; no automatic game installation.

Installed: BC1, BP2, CJ2, CP1, EG5, SB3, SS3, RG2, GC3, GT4. GC3 fitted to 500ms windup; other selections exact.
