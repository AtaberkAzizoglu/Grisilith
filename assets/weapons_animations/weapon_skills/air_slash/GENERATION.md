### Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset: transparent game animation sprite sheet. Draw completely original fresh Longsword wind slash artwork, not copies of an existing bitmap. Blue, cyan and brilliant white palette. A right-facing crescent ")" blade of rushing air, long tapered upper and lower points, hollow concave left side, convex right leading edge. CLEAN curved sword-sweep silhouette with a narrow bright white edge, translucent deep blue interior and two curling cyan wind ribbons. Not feathered, not wing-shaped, not crystalline, no tangled crisscross web. Wisps and highlights flow around the arc in DIFFERENT patterns each frame. Change ribbon positions and internal eddies as it travels. Hand-painted fantasy VFX, sophisticated controlled glow.
STRICT technical layout: a square sheet consisting of EXACTLY FOUR rows and FOUR columns, SIXTEEN EQUAL SQUARE cells. Every cell center at exactly 12.5%,37.5%,62.5%,87.5% of image width and height. Each cell has a huge empty margin: every sprite including ALL glow/trails must fit within ONLY the CENTRAL 50% of cell height and CENTRAL 40% of cell width. Fully TRANSPARENT blank bands between cells. Do not enlarge sprites to fill the sheet. NO particles outside that small central rectangle. All frames face right, no tilt, fixed center. 
Read left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Frames 1 to 8 grow gradually: heights relative to each cell 10%,16%,22%,28%,34%,40%,46%,50%. Frames 9 to 14 remain exactly 50% cell-height while inner ribbon motion and bright edge highlights change, NOT duplicate images. Frames 15 and 16 fade and unravel slightly but stay the same centered size. Do not grow past 50%. A cohesive smooth time sequence. Genuine transparent alpha, no backdrop, no checkerboard, no grid, no labels/numbers/text, no characters or physical swords.
