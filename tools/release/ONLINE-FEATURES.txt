GRISILITH — ONLINE FEATURES (BETA)

Offline gameplay is available. Internet connectivity enables the online profile,
display name, leaderboard and Praise features, using the project's Supabase service.

Online play can send an account identifier, display name, run start/result data,
map, character, score, run duration, equipment/build and gameplay summary statistics.
The leaderboard publicly displays the relevant run/name information and Praise counts.
Run/build summaries are also intended to help improve gameplay and performance.
Session credentials are stored locally and must not be shared with other players.
Connecting to an online service also exposes normal network metadata to that service.

Offline or otherwise ineligible runs do not enter the leaderboard. Do not use
personal/contact information as your public display name.

This is a practical feature disclosure, not a complete jurisdiction-specific privacy
policy. Before public distribution, the publisher must provide appropriate contact,
retention and deletion information and confirm the applicable privacy requirements.
