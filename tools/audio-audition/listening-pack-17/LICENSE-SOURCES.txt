EDITS
Mono 44.1kHz PCM16 WAV; excerpts/foreground selection, pitch/speed changes,
EQ/low-pass/high-pass, short fades; optional one animal vocal + impact/air texture.
Bee swarm designs mix time/pitch-shifted copies of a single bee recording and fade
the swarm away for death (bees do not have a vocal death cry).
Peak matched to -12dBFS; not perceived loudness matched.
Five options per group are sound designs, not five unrelated recording sessions.
Full recipes in SECENEKLER.txt and PrepareBeastCombatAudition.kt.
