EDIT DISCLOSURE
Mono 44.1kHz/16-bit WAV; excerpts, speed/pitch, reverse, low/high-pass, layered/echo offsets,
peak match ~-12dBFS, 5ms end fade. Not perceptual loudness matching or listening-quality approval.
Detailed recipes: tools/audio-audition/qa/PrepareSummonGlyphAudition.kt.
Most designs are variations/layers from these sources, not 60 unrelated original performances.
Singularity is fictional sound design, not a literal space recording or scientific sonification.
Tsunami/Meteor similarly are layered water/wind/thunder/impact designs, not genuine disaster recordings.
No real religious prayer text is intentionally used; male speech is reversed or synthetic syllables.
Casting examples are auditions; timing will be adapted to actual drawing/channel durations after approval.
The jFLAC preparation-only decoder is documented in magic-refinement-source-credits.txt; not shipped.
