Author's requested attribution: "Whipping sound by Tuomo Untinen"
Free commercial redistribution is allowed under the attribution license, not public domain.
WP5 was chosen; this attribution was added to the game's credits and shipped audio credits.

Processing: converted to mono 44.1 kHz / 16-bit WAV; peak matched to approximately -12 dBFS;
5 ms end fade. Speed/pitch edits, doubled strokes and layered recordings are labeled in the UI.
WH uses two original hammer recordings and three designed variations, not five original recordings.
WP uses one original leather-belt whip recording and four designed variations, not five original recordings.
SH uses metal sword-clash recordings suggested as shield textures, not actual shield recordings.
LN uses air swishes/whooshes suggested for spear thrusts, not actual spear impact recordings.
Sources and processing are documented in SECENEKLER.txt and the preparation code.
Source web pages are retained under sources/<pack>/source-page.html for provenance.
