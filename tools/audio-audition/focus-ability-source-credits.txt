EDITS:
Excerpts, mono/16-bit/44.1kHz conversion, speed/pitch changes, low/high-pass, reversal, layers,
gust envelopes, 5ms end fade, peak match -12dBFS. Detailed recipes in PrepareFocusAbilityAudition.kt.
Loop stems use ~83ms wrap crossfades plus a short boundary repair; preview demos contain two cycles.
Short audition periods are NOT final spell durations. Some use repeated recording textures,
not 35 unique original recordings. Sound quality/character still requires user selection.
IG stems separate teleport from arrival burst so runtime can sync to actual teleport animation.
BL is only the burn layer: no Acid Bubble/AP selected recording baked in.
BL is designed from fire/boiling/hiss recordings, not literal burning-liquid field recordings.
ER/PS dark layers are synthetic, not human voices. Low-pass processing favors a low, non-shrill tone.

The jFLAC decoder is preview-task-only, not shipped. Its LGPL2.1 details are recorded in
magic-refinement-source-credits.txt.
