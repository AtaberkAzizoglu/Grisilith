Processing: mono 44.1kHz / 16-bit WAV, peak matching around -12 dBFS, 5 ms end fade.
Warhammer options use labeled low-pass filtering, speed/pitch lowering and recorded impact layers;
they are five designed options, not five original hammer recordings. No synthesized tones added.
Dagger, Sling and Boomerang use labeled swish/foley designs, not literal recordings of those weapons.
Existing confirmed weapon sounds are NOT re-used as identical output files for these new weapons.
Sources and timing are listed in SECENEKLER.txt and the preparation code.
